About The Care Takers

Our Story

Founded in 2015, The Care Takers Pvt Ltd has emerged as Pakistan's leading facilities management company. Our journey began with a simple vision: to deliver premium facility services with uncompromising quality and professionalism.

Today, we serve over 500+ corporate clients and organizations across Pakistan, providing comprehensive cleaning, maintenance, and technical services. Our team of highly trained professionals is committed to exceeding expectations in every interaction.

We believe that excellent facility management is the foundation of productive, healthy, and thriving workplaces. This philosophy drives every decision we make and every service we deliver.
